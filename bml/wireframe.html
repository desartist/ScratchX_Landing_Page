Day by day

Trip itinerary

Arrive in Bali and transfer to a serene Ubud resort surrounded by rice paddies. Relax into the calming atmosphere before joining a gentle fitness session to ease travel fatigue. Conclude the day with a warm welcome dinner in a scenic nature setting, bonding with fellow travelers.
Begin with a sunrise yoga flow overlooking the valley, then explore Ubud's cultural heart — local markets, temples and an art village — before an evening of traditional Balinese cuisine.
A guided breathwork session is followed by a refreshing trek to Tegenungan waterfall. Wind down in the evening with a restorative sound-healing ceremony under the stars.
Morning movement on the sand, then head to the coast for snorkelling among vibrant reefs. The afternoon is yours to swim, rest or chase the perfect sunset.
Visit the lakeside Ulun Danu temple, then surrender to a traditional Balinese massage. The trip closes with a heartfelt group dinner and reflection circle.
A final gentle practice and slow breakfast before your transfer to the airport — carrying home new habits, friendships and stories.
